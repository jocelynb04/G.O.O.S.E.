Concept 1 propulsion trade
All cases use 9,000 lbf takeoff weight, 2,430 lbf payload, a 64.9-ft span,
aspect ratio 7.2, the 1,000-statute-mile mission, and 45-min reserve loiter.

Case 1: Original propulsion assumptions at the shorter-span geometry.
Case 2: Twin PT6A-21 reference with the original electric rating.
Case 3: Same engine pair. Electric rating from the current water-takeoff
correlation with the design power margin, about 351 shp total.

Engine references
FAA E4EA pp. 3-4: 550 shp per engine at sea level for takeoff and max
continuous, 337 lb dry per engine, 61.89-in length.
https://www.faa.gov/sites/faa.gov/files/training_testing/testing/supplements/FAA-CT-8080-8D.pdf
Pratt and Whitney Canada maintenance manual 3013242 section 72-00-00,
Table 1: 0.630 lb/(eshp hr) at 580 eshp and 550 shp, converted to
0.664 lb/(shp hr). The study uses this sea-level static JP-4 point as a
constant sensitivity input. Cruise and altitude fuel performance require a map.
https://pusi.vilsystem.com/MM_PT6/72-00.pdf
The 1.4 installation factor remains a screening approximation. The model
uses flat altitude power, a preliminary water correlation, and a pack-level
energy density of 250 Wh/kg. The 6C example borrows the Cessna hybrid study
limit. It illustrates the effect of a discharge constraint on battery mass.
Balked landing, recharge, and pack thermal performance require analysis.
